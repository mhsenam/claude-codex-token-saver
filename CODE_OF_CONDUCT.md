# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

TokenSaver is a small, technical project. Be constructive: critique code, not people. Keep discussions focused on the software, stay patient with newcomers, and respect that maintainers volunteer their time.

The following are unacceptable in any project space (issues, pull requests, discussions):

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks
- Trolling or deliberately derailing threads
- Publishing others' private information without consent
- Sexualized language or imagery
- Other conduct a reasonable person would consider inappropriate

## Scope

This applies to the project's repository spaces and to any communications representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainer: mhsenamm@gmail.com. Reports are reviewed confidentially. Violations may result in edited or removed comments, or a ban from project spaces.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this code in good faith may face temporary or permanent removal from project leadership.
